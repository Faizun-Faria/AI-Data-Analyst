# Personas

## Complaints operations lead

Runs the team that handles incoming complaints and reports on them to senior management.

- **Goals:** spot rising issues early; report volumes and response timeliness each month; show that the numbers hold up when questioned.
- **Frustrations:** every question becomes a ticket to the data team; reports built by different people disagree; nobody can say exactly how a figure was produced.
- **Typical questions:**
  - "How many credit card complaints did we get last quarter, and what share got a timely response?"
  - "Which issues grew fastest this year?"
  - "Show me the monthly trend for billing disputes."
- **Needs from Complaint Radar:** a fast answer in plain English, in the right format (a number, a chart or a short summary), with a definition they can quote.

## Risk analyst

Works in a second-line risk or compliance function, looking for patterns of customer harm and testing whether an issue is emerging.

- **Goals:** detect emerging conduct risk; compare a firm against its peers; build evidence for a review.
- **Frustrations:** raw data is messy and categories change over time; someone else's figure can't be reproduced; tools give confident answers without showing their working.
- **Typical questions:**
  - "Were unauthorised-account complaints rising at a given company before a given date?"
  - "How does this company's share of mortgage complaints compare with its five largest peers?"
  - "Which companies have the lowest timely-response rate for debt collection?"
- **Needs from Complaint Radar:** the SQL, row count and assumptions behind every answer, so they can check and re-run it; a clarifying question when the ask is ambiguous, rather than a guess.
